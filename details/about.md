About Realty Launch
Your Gateway to Smarter Real Estate Decisions
At Realty Launch, we believe that finding the right property is more than just buying land or a home — it's about discovering opportunities, building a secure future, and making confident investment decisions.
We are a real estate marketing and property discovery platform focused on showcasing residential plots, villas, flats, gated community projects, and land investment opportunities across Hyderabad and emerging locations in Telangana.
Our goal is to make property discovery simpler, more informative, and more accessible by connecting property seekers with opportunities that match their aspirations, preferences, and investment goals.
What We Do
At Realty Launch, we bring property opportunities closer to you through digital marketing, engaging property videos, informative content, and personalised enquiry assistance.
Our services include:
Residential Plots: Discover open plots in residential layouts and gated communities.
Villas & Flats: Explore residential properties suited to different lifestyles and budgets.
HMDA, DTCP & RERA-Related Projects: Discover projects with relevant planning approvals and registrations, subject to project-specific verification.
Land Investment Opportunities: Explore developing locations and emerging real estate destinations around Hyderabad and Telangana.
Digital Property Marketing: We leverage social media, video content, and digital marketing strategies to showcase properties and connect interested buyers with property opportunities.

Meet the Founder
Shashank Reddy
Founder – Realty Launch
Realty Launch was founded by Shashank Reddy, a real estate professional, digital marketer, and content creator with a passion for bringing technology and innovation into the real estate industry.
With experience in real estate marketing, digital strategy, and content creation, Shashank envisioned Realty Launch as a platform that bridges the gap between property opportunities and potential buyers through informative content and effective digital marketing.
His approach combines industry understanding with modern digital marketing strategies to make property discovery more engaging, accessible, and informative.
Driven by an entrepreneurial mindset and a passion for exploring new opportunities, he aims to build Realty Launch into a recognised name in digital real estate marketing and property discovery.
His vision is simple: Make property discovery easier, smarter, and more accessible for everyone.
